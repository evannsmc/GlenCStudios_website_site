Welcome to GlenC Art Studio

What began as a personal journey toward healing and balance during a time when my mind and body felt exhausted gradually became a creative outlet—and rekindled my passion for art.

Through color, movement, patterns, and intuitive expression, I began to loosen my grip on perfection and embrace experimentation and discovery. Along the way, I learned that creativity doesn't need to be perfect to be meaningful.

In my paintings, I explore how colors interact to create movement, evoke emotions, and establish a sense of balance. I approach each piece intuitively, allowing it to develop in its own way.

I hope my art offers you an opportunity to pause, reflect, and discover your own connection with what you see. For me, this studio is more than a place where art is created; it is a space for exploration, reflection, and creative freedom.

Thank you for being here!

With color, movement, and heart,GlenC
